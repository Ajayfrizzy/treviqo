# Treviqo AI Documents

Read `/docs/ai/AI_DOCUMENTS.md`, `/docs/security/SECURITY.md`, `/docs/domain/DOMAIN_RULES.md`, and `/AGENTS.md`.

Assume a relatively small open-source inference model.

Use narrow, schema-driven prompts and strict structured output.

AI may classify and extract explicit evidence.

AI must not make legal judgments, invent facts, or determine entitlement.

Important extracted fields remain proposed until confirmed or corrected by the user.

Use synthetic fixtures/mocks for automated tests so live inference credits are not repeatedly consumed.
